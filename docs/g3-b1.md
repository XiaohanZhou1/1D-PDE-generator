# G3 audit and B1 Bloch calibration

## G3 decisions

The shared workflow validates each generated record against its schema and reconstructs the exact canonical specification. Its audit sidecar records applicability of family-specific checks, dispatch eligibility, a proof shortcut screen, novelty provenance and the result's evidence grade. The existing A1 and A5 specification hashes and fixtures are compatible with this layer.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| VALIDATED | The contract passed and this run requests mathematical audit only. |
| REJECTED | Contract or canonical reconstruction failed. The candidate JSON and reason are retained. |
| DUPLICATE | The exact mathematical hash already occurred in the batch. The row links to that canonical instance. |
| HOLD | The specification is valid but lies outside the backend's enabled capability. |
| UNCERTAIN | The solver failed or did not return sufficient evidence. |
| EVALUATED | The backend returned a label with sufficient evidence for its stated task. |

The A5 sampler keeps the three existing anchors and chooses the other two parameters on a rational grid from a seed. G3 reports an A5 pair within \(1/8\) as nearby within its structure and fixed \(\rho\). The report is advisory. Exact hashes control deduplication, while the family group keeps nearby cases together in any later split. The A1 decoupled control and B1 examples have known reductions. The A5 proof screen records conservative integration and the origin analysis as existing methods, with finite-frequency proof novelty left to expert review. Each case remains on hold for proof-training admission. A5 assigns **CONVERGED_NUMERICAL** after checking the target PDE residual and the resolved outer and origin windings. Rejected solver conclusions retain their native records.

## B1 periodic rolls

Use \(X_t=X_{xx}+X-(X^\top X)X\), with the real two-component stationary roll

$$
U(x)=\sqrt{a}(\cos qx,\sin qx)^\top,\qquad a=1-q^2,\quad L=2\pi/q.
$$

The signed spectrum is taken on the full unweighted line. A Bloch fiber uses periodic complexified components on the cell of length \(L\), with derivative \(\partial_x+i\xi\) and \(-q/2\le \xi<q/2\). Substitution gives the profile residual \((1-a-q^2)U=0\). Differentiating the nonlinear term gives potential \(q^2I-2UU^\top\), and \(U_x\) is a phase eigenfunction in the \(\xi=0\) fiber.

Rotating by \(R(qx)\) reduces the fibers to Hermitian Fourier blocks at \(k=mq+\xi\), \(m\in\mathbb Z\). The two eigenvalues are

$$
\lambda_\pm(k)=-k^2-a\pm\sqrt{a^2+4q^2k^2}.
$$

For \(k\ne0\) the upper branch has the sign of \(6q^2-2-k^2\). For the full line the union of fibers covers all real \(k\). For co-periodic perturbations the allowed values are integer multiples of \(q\), including the translation zero. At \(q^2=1/3\) the upper branch is nonpositive and approaches zero at fourth order. This pilot calls that threshold **MARGINAL** to retain the distinction from a quadratic phase edge. [Guillod et al., Nonlinear stability at the Eckhaus boundary](https://doi.org/10.1137/18M1174751)

| Exact squared wavenumber | Co-periodic | Full line |
|---:|---|---|
| \(1/4\) | STABLE modulo phase | STABLE |
| \(9/25\) | STABLE modulo phase | UNSTABLE |
| \(1/3\) | STABLE modulo phase | MARGINAL |

Here STABLE means spectral exclusion of positive real part under the stated phase convention. The full-line phase band touches zero even on the stable side. At \(q=3/5\) the unstable band includes \(0<|k|<2/5\), so the first nonzero co-periodic mode at \(|k|=3/5\) misses it. The finite-difference check discretizes the physical two-component operator with twisted difference shifts on 64, 128 and 256 nodes. It compares three leading eigenvalues on the zero fiber and one nonzero test fiber against the dispersion formula. The analytic sign argument covers the entire Bloch zone. The numerical checks corroborate the sampled fibers. All B1 cases are calibration cases and share the same proof mechanism.

## Reproduce

Run from the repository root in the environment specified in the README.

~~~powershell
$env:OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS = '1'
python -m pdegen.workflow --family A1 --out scratch/g3-a1
python -m pdegen.workflow --family A5 --out scratch/g3-a5
python -m pdegen.workflow --family B1 --out scratch/b1
python -m pdegen.generate --family B1 --q2 9/25 --out scratch/b1-export
~~~

A1 runs the G3 contract and novelty checks. A5 uses bundled twstab and B1 uses a dedicated Bloch calibration backend. The B1 specification has its own versioned JSON schema with a common audit layer. General periodic profiles and nonconstant-coefficient Bloch continuation are outside this pilot.
