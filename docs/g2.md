## Mathematical checks

With $g=1-u^2,\ H=u^2/2+5u^3/24,\ h=v-H(u)$ and $k=\mu(1+u/2)$, the original flux is $\mathcal F=(g+kh,\ H'g+(H'k+u)h+\rho g)^T$. Along $U=(\tanh x,H(\tanh x))^T$, $h=0$ and $U'=\mathcal F(U)$. The generator substitutes and differentiates over exact rationals to check the travelling-wave equation, this first integral, positive phase slope, zero wave speed, the Rankine–Hugoniot identity and zero flux at both limits $(-1,7/24),(1,17/24)$.

For $J=\mathcal F'(U)$ the generator derives $Lp=p''-(Jp)'$, including both $B=-J$ and $C=-J'$, and verifies $LU'=0$. With $z=\tanh x$, it checks that the discriminant of the computed physical Jacobian equals

$$
9z^2+4\mu(1+z/2)(1+5z/4)(1-z^2).
$$

For $z\in[-1,-4/5]$, the first term is at least $144/25$ and the possible negative contribution has magnitude at most $54/25$. On $[-4/5,1]$ the terms are nonnegative, and the second is positive at $z=0$, the sole zero of the first. This proves strict hyperbolicity along the full compact profile curve. The computed limiting trace and determinant give left characteristic speeds $2,-1$ and right speeds $-2,1$, so exactly one characteristic enters from each side. Both limits have $C=0$. The resulting dispersion parabolas give a $2+2$ spatial split in the open right half-plane. The shock convention permits the essential edge at zero and a simple continued translation zero.

The original profile unfolding also follows from $\bar h'=u\bar h+\rho g$ in the integrated profile ODE. At $\rho=0$, the first variation is $\dot h'=\bar u\dot h+g(\bar u)$, where $\bar u=\tanh x$. Multiplying by $\operatorname{sech}x$ gives the nonzero splitting integral $\int_{\mathbb R}\operatorname{sech}^3 x\,dx=\pi/2$. This applies to the reviewed flux structure across the branch. The solver separately checks outgoing-mode compatibility at the spectral origin for each parameter. [A5 construction and spectral convention](mathematics.md#a5-contract-fixture)
